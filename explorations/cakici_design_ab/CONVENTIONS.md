# Exploration: Cakici A/B Decile Construction Prototype

This is a research prototype exploring two design choices for the explicit ADV liquidity gate in DRIF decile construction (issue #312). The code in this directory is at exploration-score 60+ and is intentionally not production-grade: no roxygen docs, no full error handling, and no wiring into the main targets pipeline. Once the design decision between Option A (filter-then-rank, paper intent) and Option B (rank-then-renormalise) is made based on the evidence produced here, the chosen variant will be implemented as a full `tar_target` entry in `R/plan_stock_backtest.R`, replacing or augmenting the current `stk_drif_portfolio` target. At that point the snapshot tests in `tests/test-variants.R` should be migrated to `packages/historicaldata/tests/testthat/` or `tests/testthat/` as appropriate.
